SCHEME NAME: "CENT HOTEL"

1. TARGET GROUP:
All Business Units who want to avail loan facility for services activities associated with Hospitality industry including Hotels, Restaurants, Cafes, etc. along with self-employed and professional individuals covered by MSMED Act 2006, modified from time to time.
Resort cum Hotel as well as Hotels to be built on leased land / premises (where mortgage of lease hold rights is permitted in favor of the Bank) from Government Agencies / IRCTC/Tourism Development Corporation of state or Central Government, etc.

2. PURPOSE:
Term loan for build-up of fixed assets needed for business purpose, capacity expansion, modernization and short term working capital for current assets.
Under Sole Banking arrangement only.

3. NATURE OF FACILITY:
Fund Based (FB) / NFB.

4. TYPE OF LOAN:
1. Term Loan
2. Over Draft (or)
3. Cash Credit
Overdraft under the scheme will be allowed in case of composite loan only and maximum up to Rs.50 lakh.
Non Fund Base facility is allowed under overall limit of Rs.100 Crore.

5. ELIGIBILITY:
- New units offering a mortgageable property as collateral security.
- Existing Customers already availing credit facilities from us.
- Takeover of existing units from other Banks/ FIs with satisfactory track record. (Credit information report as per the extant Master Circular on Credit Information Companies (CICs) to be obtained)
- In case of HUF and Trust with 100% collateral security.
- UDHYAM Registration Certificate is mandatory.

6. LOAN AMOUNT:
For all locations- Rural/Semi-Urban/Urban/Metro:
- Minimum loan amount: Rs.10 Lakh
- Maximum loan amount: Rs.100 Crore
- Maximum working capital limit is Rs.5.00 crore, within maximum exposure of Rs.100.00 Crore.
- Loan up to Rs.2.00 Crore may be extended under CGTMSE guarantee cover.
- Total composite loan should not exceed Rs.100.00 Crore under the scheme.

7. INTEREST RATE:
For Loan of Rs.10 Lakh - RBLR + 0.95 % p.a.
For Loan above Rs.10 Lakh:
- If Internal Rating is CBI-1 to CBI-3:
  * If market Value of Collateral Security is equal to at least 75% of exposure: RBLR
  * If market Value of Collateral Security is equal to at least 50% of exposure: RBLR+0.10%
  * If market Value of Collateral Security is Less than 50% of exposure & in respect of CGTMSE guarantee covered accounts: RBLR+0.20%
- If Internal Rating is CBI-4 to CBI-6:
  * If market Value of Collateral Security is equal to at least 75% of exposure: RBLR+0.10%
  * If market Value of Collateral Security is equal to at least 50% of exposure: RBLR+0.20%
  * If market Value of Collateral Security is Less than 50% of exposure & in respect of CGTMSE guarantee covered accounts: RBLR+0.30%
Note: Collateral Security includes Land and Building only. Plant and Machinery will not be included here.

Tenor Premium for term loans (inclusive of Moratorium):
- For Term loan upto 3 years: NIL
- Term Loan above 3 years to 5 years: 0.20%
- Term Loan above 5 years to 7 years: 0.30%
- Above 7 years: 0.50%

Concession on Interest Rate (may be approved by sanctioning authority on basis of collateral security offered):
- Amount of collateral Security >=50% to <=60%: 0.50% concession
- Amount of collateral Security >60% to <=75%: 0.60% concession
- Amount of collateral Security >75%: 0.75% concession
* Note: The land on which hotel is proposed to be constructed may be taken as collateral security, if the land cost is not part of project cost.
* Final rate of Interest after concession, if any, should not go below the RBLR.
* Penal charges: In case of any default in payment of interest/instalment, penal charges is to be charged @ 2% over sanctioned interest rate for the overdue amount and for overdue period.

8. SANCTIONING AUTHORITY:
As per delegated lending powers.

9. SECURITY:
Primary:
- Assets created with the loan amount (both fixed & current)
Collateral:
- Collateral security of property should be mortgage able.
- Minimum Collateral security including land cost should cover 50% of the loan amount.
- In case of HUF and trust 100% collateral should be taken but the property should not be in the name of HUF and Trust.
- Immovable property (compliant under SARFAESI Act) belonging to the unit, its proprietor/partners/directors or their close relatives. Close relatives will cover Father, Mother, Spouse, Son, Daughter, real Brother & real Sister.
- Immovable property in the form of Equitable / Registered Mortgage of land and building, by way of exclusive charge.
- No Second Charge or Pari-Passu charge will be extended for other Bank/FI.
- Further, security which will be provided as collateral should not be linked with other loan / liabilities i.e. collateral property should be exclusive to this loan. Confirmation to this effect should be recorded in the proposal.
- CERSAI verification shall be done on the collateral Securities.
- The land on which hotel is proposed to be constructed may be taken as collateral security, if the land cost is not part of project cost.

CGTMSE Cover:
- Loan upto Rs.2.00 Crore may be extended under CGTMSE guarantee cover.
Leasehold rights:
- Mortgage of lease hold rights in favor of the Bank provided the same cannot be cancelled during the loan period.

10. MARGIN: 25%

APPLICABLE RATIOS (as per Master Credit Policy):
Term Loan:
- Debt equity ratio: 4:1 (for loans up to Rs. 1 cr) | 3:1 (for loans above Rs. 1 cr)
- Average Debt Coverage service ratio (DSCR): 1.25
- Asset coverage ratio: 1.33
- TOL/ATNW (After taking quasi capital up to 100% of TNW): 5:1
Working Capital:
- Current Ratio: 1:1 (for loans up to Rs. 1 cr) | 1.10:1 (for loans above Rs. 1 cr)
- Interest coverage ratio: 1.50
- Asset coverage ratio: 1.33

11. ASSESSMENT OF THE CREDIT LIMITS:
- The need based assessment for the requirement of credit limits has to be done.
Working Capital:
- Upto Rs.5 Crore, working capital credit limit to be computed as per Turnover Method.
- Borrower will submit cash flow for the entire tenor of loan, at the time of initial sanction of limits and annually thereafter at the time of review of the limits.
Term Loan:
- Fixed Assets to be financed based on the actual expenditure proposed.
- Term Loan to be assessed as per Cash flow with minimum average DSCR of 1.25.

12. REPAYMENT PERIOD:
1. Term Loan - Maximum 240 Months (Post COD, moratorium of maximum 24 months as per project requirement may be allowed).
2. Working Capital - 12 Months (subject to review / renewal every year). No over-drawings permitted.

13. DISBURSEMENT:
- The disbursement to be made directly to the supplier/vendor/contractor, etc.
- The disbursements should be made in stages depending on progress of work and end use verification report should be on record.
- A Certificate to be obtained from Architect/Engineer on periodical basis.
- Working capital facility sanctioned, if any, should be released only after completion of construction and on commencement of business.

14. TAKE OVER CRITERIA:
Takeover of the existing units are allowed as per the extant Takeover norms.

15. PRE-PAYMENT PENALTY:
- 4% of the outstanding in the account, in case of Takeover.
- In case of pre-payment / pre-closure from internal accruals, pre-payment / pre-closure penalty will not be levied.

16. LSR (LEGAL SEARCH REPORT):
- Legal Search Report should be obtained from Bank's Panel Advocate. All actions for obtaining legal search report from Panel Advocate should be carried out by the branch directly and borrower should not be advised to meet the Advocate.
- Certificate confirming genuineness of the title deeds and certified copies of title deeds should be obtained from the Advocate.
- All other conditions of SOP on LSR to be fulfilled.

17. VALUATION OF PROPERTY:
- Valuation of collateral property to be carried out as per the Bank's guidelines.
- Fresh valuation of property to be carried out every 3 years.

18. TEV (TECHNO ECONOMIC VIABILITY):
- TEV reports to be taken for loan above Rs. 10.00 Crore as per norms.

19. DUE DILIGENCE:
Due Diligence of the borrower:
- Satisfactory evidence of proof of residence.
- Pre inspection of residence, business, offices, with an element of surprise.
- Business details to be cross checked.
- Borrower history of the borrower/CO-borrower to be thoroughly examined.
Due Diligence of the property to be mortgaged:
- The property should be well identifiable, well demarcated and boundarised, marketable.
- The property should be easily accessible and saleable.
- In case of Plot/land, it must be well demarcated and boundarised.
- CERSAI registration report to be generated and details to be verified to avoid multiple financing.
- Due Diligence by external agency to be obtained as per norms.

20. INSPECTION:
- Quarterly inspection of property and unit/activity should be conducted and report with photographs should be kept on record for audit purpose.

21. INTERNAL RATING:
- All the proposals up to Rs.2 Cr shall be rated manually using revised MSME Scoring model.
- Proposals above Rs.2 Cr shall be rated on RAM rating model.
- Hurdle score for Fresh sanction is CBI VI and for Takeover CBI V under the scheme.

22. CIBIL CMR SCORE:
- In case of individuals, CIBIL/CRIF score of the individual should be as per extant guidelines. (At present CIBIL-700, EXPERIAN-700, EQUIFAX-700 and CRIF HIGH MARK- 650).
- For Proprietorship/Partnership/Companies, CIBIL CMR Rank should be checked. However CMR ranking may not be considered for decision taking.
- 2 CICs to be obtained for loans above Rs 50 lakh and where borrower is new- to - credit (NTC) where CIC score is -1 or no credit history.
- Minimum CIBIL score of the applicant should be 700 and exception shall be permitted as under:
  * Zonal Head (ZH) (GM/DGM): Below 700 and up to 675
  * Vertical Head (GM) at CO: Below 675 and up to 650
- Facilities are to be sanctioned by respective sanctioning authorities as per delegated lending powers. Approval for CIC score only shall be under the purview as mentioned above. The above relaxation in CIC score is not applicable to CRIF High Mark.

23. PROCESSING CHARGES / UPFRONT FEE:
- Fresh Sanction: 1% of the sanctioned limit (Maximum of Rs.2.00 lakh) + GST
- Review / renewal: 0.25% (Maximum of Rs.0.50 lakh) of the limit + GST

24. MORTGAGE CHARGES:
- Rs.10 Lakh: Rs.2000/-
- Above Rs.10 Lakh - Rs.1 Crore: Rs.75/ per Lakh (Minimum Rs.2000/- and Maximum Rs.7500/-)
- Above Rs.1 Crore: Rs.50/ per Lakh (Minimum Rs.7500/- and Maximum Rs.35,000/-).

25. INSPECTION CHARGES:
- Limits upto Rs.1 Cr: Rs.1000/- per inspection quarterly.
- Limits above Rs.1 Cr: Rs. 3000/- per inspection quarterly.

26. COMMITMENT CHARGES (If Availment is):
- Less than 50% = 1% p.a.
- 50% to < 60% = 0.50% p.a.
- 60% to < 70% = 0.25% p.a.
- > 70% = NIL

27. CERSAI:
- Valid Equitable/Registered mortgage of the property to be created and Charge with CERSAI to be created before release of limits.

28. CERSAI CHARGES:
- 150/- + GST

29. CIC CHARGES:
- Consumer: @Rs. 50/- + GST per CIC
- Commercial: @Rs. 800/- + GST per CIC

30. DOCUMENTATION:
- Loan Documents
- Mortgage of property
- LSR should be obtained from two different panel advocates in case of loan is above Rs 1 Crore.
- Mortgager should have absolute, clear and marketable title as on the date of mortgage.

31. THIRD PARTY GUARANTEE:
- Personal Guarantee from the Promoters / Partners / Directors of the Unit, Karta/ Coparceners in case of HUF and Trustees in case of Trust.
- Personal Guarantee of the owner of the Collateral Security offered for Mortgage.

32. GUARANTEE COVER:
- Loan upto Rs. 2.00 Crore may be extended under CGTMSE guarantee cover.

33. ENHANCEMENT IN LIMIT:
- Can be considered only after one year of date of sanction subject to the following:
  * Internal Rating must not be downgraded.
  * Against additional collateral security (compliance with SARFAESI act) provided by the unit and/or surplus available in existing collateral securities (within the permissible LTV level) subject to cash flow being sufficient to take care of repayment in the residual period (tenor will not be increased).
  * No enhancement will be considered for SMA accounts (Account should not have been classified SMA-1 & 2 since last review of the account).

34. UNDERTAKING FROM THE BORROWER:
- Yearly affidavit from the borrower that funds have been / will be utilized for the approved activities under MSMED Act 2006 and not used for speculative purpose like investment in stock market, acquisition/development of land, etc., or for any activity not permitted by law.

35. END USE OF FUNDS:
- End use of funds to be ensured immediately upon disbursement of loan, through inspection/verification of purchase bills, etc. In addition, QOD cum status of working capital funds statement to be obtained from the borrower to confirm the level of current assets and end use of funds.

36. INSURANCE:
- Insurance cover, both for the assets of the activity for which CENT HOTEL has been sanctioned and for the property against which loan is given should be taken, as in the case of regular finance.
- Primary security created with the Bank's Finance to be fully insured with Bank's Hypothecation Clause preferably through our channel partners.
- Collateral security to be fully insured with Bank's Hypothecation Clause preferably through our channel partners.

37. ROUTING OF TRANSACTIONS:
- Transactions relating to the activity for which finance under is granted should be routed only through the Overdraft account maintained with the branch.

38. SERVICE CHARGES:
- Other Service Charges will be applicable, as per Bank's extant guidelines.

39. LEGAL AUDIT:
- Bank's extant norms with regard to legal audit will be adhered to.

40. APPRAISAL FORMAT:
- Common Appraisal Memorandum (IBA - Format)

41. CLASSIFICATION UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR:
- As per extant guidelines of MSME classification.

42. DEVIATIONS IN THE SCHEME:
- Deviation under the scheme – HLCC-GM.

43. OTHER TERMS & CONDITIONS:
1. Unit should be registered under MSMED act and should provide Udyam Registration Certificate.
2. The borrowing entity should possess all required permissions/approvals/licenses for carrying out the proposed activity.
3. In case of term loans for purchase/acquisition of fixed assets, disbursement should be made directly to the supplier and tax paid invoices should be held on record.
4. Inspection of the machinery/assets to be carried out and inspection report to be held on record.
5. Genuineness of the supplier/vendor to be ensured at the time of term loan disbursement.
6. Borrower should maintain their exclusive account with our bank and all their operations should be routed through us.
7. Accessibility and Marketability of the unit should be ensured.
8. Loan to be disbursed only after obtaining CGTMSE coverage, in case of loan is sanctioned under CGTMSE guarantee cover.
9. Concession in processing charges will be as per MSME Loan policy.
10. In case of Company Registration of charges with MCA to be done.
11. CERSAI registration should be done.
12. Current account, if any, maintained with other Bank shall be closed.
13. Applicable licenses to be obtained before disbursement of the loan.
14. Facilities related to Merchant Establishment and QR code should be extended in all cases.
